Privacy policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what Grade Lab needs to work: your account details if you create one, and your revision progress.
  • We don't show adverts, we don't use tracking or analytics tools, and we never sell your data.
  • You can use Grade Lab without an account. Your progress then stays in your browser on that device.
  • You can delete your account, and everything linked to it, at any time from Profile → Settings.

Who we are

Grade Lab (gradelab.netlify.app) is a GCSE revision app run by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact us about anything in this policy at gradelab26@gmail.com.

Grade Lab is intended for students aged 13 and over.

Our legal bases

We process account and progress data because it's necessary to provide the service you've asked for (contract). We use bot protection on sign-up and sign-in, and keep basic server logs, because we have a legitimate interest in keeping Grade Lab secure and working.

Data stored on your device

Grade Lab uses your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies. It holds your progress, settings (such as sound on or off), your study activity history, and a sign-in token if you're logged in. The app also keeps a cached copy of itself, and of past papers you've opened, so it works offline. This storage is strictly necessary for the app to work, and it stays on your device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings. If you don't have an account, doing that erases your progress.

Who else processes your data

We use a small number of service providers to run Grade Lab. They only process data on our behalf, to provide their service:

  • Supabase: sign-in and the database that stores accounts, progress and the leaderboard.
  • Netlify: hosts the website and the small server functions it uses, with standard server logs such as IP address and request times.
  • Stripe: takes payments for full access. Stripe handles your card details directly; we never see or store them. Stripe tells us the email address and whether a payment succeeded, so we can switch on full access for your account.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: checks that sign-ups and sign-ins come from a person, not a bot. It looks at information about your browser and device.
  • Google Fonts: serves the typefaces on some pages, so your browser contacts Google's servers, which see your IP address.
  • Anthropic: only where AI marking is available and you use it (see below).

When you open a past paper inside Grade Lab, our server fetches the PDF from the exam board's official website (AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR or WJEC Eduqas) and passes it to you. The exam board doesn't receive your details.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on recognised legal safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

AI marking

Where AI marking is available, and only when you press the button to use it, we send three things to Anthropic's API: the exam question, its mark scheme, and the answer you typed. Anthropic returns a suggested mark and feedback. We don't send your name or email. Please don't type personal information into your answers. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models. AI feedback can be wrong, so treat it as a guide and check it against the mark scheme.

How long we keep data

  • Account data, progress and your leaderboard entry: until you delete your account.
  • Server logs: for the limited period our hosting provider keeps them.
  • Data on your device: until you clear it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access your data and to have it corrected or deleted. You can also ask us to restrict or stop processing it, and ask for a copy in a portable format. You can delete your account yourself from Profile → Settings. That removes your account, progress and leaderboard entry. For anything else, email gradelab26@gmail.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Children

Grade Lab is built for GCSE students, and we collect only the minimum needed to run the app. There's no advertising, no profiling for marketing, and no public profile beyond the optional leaderboard name. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before buying full access.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app.
